The call, word for word

The owner is fictional. Her lines were spoken by a local synthetic voice and read back by speech-to-text, so this is what the software heard. After the fixed opening, the assistant's lines are model replies, recorded once, checked by the output guard and frozen. Nothing on this page calls a model.

    What runs when you click

    The same code as the replay command. It reads the words of her last answer, writes to a mock CRM, reads the contact back, then tries the text and the callback. Your click changes the words it reads. The result is worked out each time.

    There is no phone line and no real CRM account behind this. Requests and responses are checked against HighLevel's published API schemas, pinned at commit . A real sub-account still has to confirm duplicate settings, notifications, and how it answers a blocked text.

    Rerun all of it, including rewordings that are not in the audio: node replay.mjs in the public repository. Once the speech model is downloaded it needs no network and no key.
